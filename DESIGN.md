## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the quiet majesty of a multi-decade botanical estate in the Western Ghats foothills. It merges the gravitas of historical botanical expeditions with contemporary luxury travel editorial design.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Serene Gravitas:** Grounded, timeless, and deliberate. Interfaces avoid restless micro-interactions and frenetic motion in favor of calm pacing, substantial presence, and archival balance.
- **Botanical Scholarship:** Communicates deep agronomic lineage and ecological custody without clinical sterility; feels like private library field notes curated for an astute guest.
- **Understated Opulence:** Luxury revealed through negative space, razor-sharp hairline borders, warm parchment typography, and tactile gold accents—never through synthetic gloss or neon gradients.

### Design Movement: Dark Botanical Editorial
The style synthesizes high-end European print journals (curated serif scales, hairline rules, wide kerning on secondary labels) with dark-mode architectural precision. Dark forest-obsidian backgrounds recede gracefully, giving priority to lush photography of rare flora, plantation canopies, and artisan produce.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict low-key luminance, evoking nightfall over canopies, fertile humus soil, and antique brass field instruments.

### Palette Construction
- **Primary Canvas (`#111311`):** A tinted carbon-green void serving as the base floor.
- **Sub-surface Canvas (`#0B0D0B`):** Recessed wells, deep backdrops, and navigation underlays.
- **Surface Elevation (`#161916`):** Elevated cards, tour panels, and tactile trays.
- **Text & Editorial Elements:**
  - **Parchment Primary (`#EAE4D8`):** High-contrast editorial headlines, essential body reads, and active controls.
  - **Muted Herbarium (`#A8A89C`):** Subheadings, descriptive copy, captions, and secondary stats.
  - **Faint Field Grey (`#6B6D66`):** Disabled states, metadata timestamps, and inactive icons.
- **Antique Gold Accent (`#B99868`):** Highlights, active badges, interactive hover fills, focus rings, and signature iconography.
- **Structural Lines & Insets:**
  - Standard structural border: `#282C28`
  - High-tier decorative border: `rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.2)`
  - Subdued partition rule: `rgba(234, 228, 216, 0.08)`

## Typography

The typography pairs historical literary prose with clean contemporary functionalism.

### Role Division & Usage
- **Editorial Headlines & Literary Accents:** Utilizes a classical humanist serif with high-contrast serifs and poetic italics for farm narratives, species classifications, and welcome statements. Headings preserve natural sentence casing or title casing without faux bolding.
- **Eyebrows, Accents & Badges:** Rendered in widely tracked uppercase. All eyebrow tags must remain between 11px and 13px with tracking set strictly between `0.12em` and `0.20em`.
- **Interface, Numbers & Narrative Body:** Clean geometric humanist sans handles interactive controls, tour bookings, operational metrics, and long-form agricultural insights. Numbers rely on tabular or proportionate figures with neutral styling.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8px base grid dictates all rhythm, structural layouts, and content distributions across responsive breakpoints.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout conforming to an inner max-width of 1360px. Outer margins sit at 48px to 64px with standard 24px to 32px gutters. Editorial spreads can use an asymmetrical 5-column (image/map) vs 7-column (story/meta) split.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. Gaps compress to 20px with 32px safe margins. Media and content blocks collapse to 4/4 splits or single-column runs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. 16px lateral canvas margins, 16px inter-column gaps. Spatial rhythm scales down to ensure botanical photography retains visual predominance.

### Compositional Cadence
Generous vertical spacing marks section transitions (`space-xl` multiplied up to 80px or 120px) to allow content to breathe, evoking the physical walking pace of the estate. Internal card padding uses a steady 24px (`1.5rem`) on desktop and 16px (`1rem`) on hand-held views.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through low-luminance surface hierarchy and razor-thin metallic boundaries rather than ambient drop shadows. Heavy blurs and diffused black shadows are avoided.

### Architectural Surface Layers
- **Floor Ground (Z-0):** `#0B0D0B` used behind canvas layers and root pages.
- **Primary Ground (Z-1):** `#111311` for baseline sections, editorial bands, and articles.
- **Raised Planes (Z-2):** `#161916` for tour cards, modal trays, interactive booking tiles, and filter bars.

### Boundary Hierarchy
1. **Structural Delimiters:** 1px stroke of `#282C28` outlines all card perimeters, horizontal rules, and table cells.
2. **Accented Relic Delimiters:** 1px stroke of `rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.2)` on high-value conversion elements, focused cards, and hero frame insets.
3. **Floating Overlays (Z-3):** Menus, bottom navigation sheets, and booking summaries utilize background fill `#161916` with 95% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, and a 1px border of `rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The architectural personality requires tight, restrained corners. Rounded corners are intentionally subtle to maintain an archival, structured silhouette.

### Corner Architecture
- **Buttons, Field Inputs, Badges:** 4px (`0.25rem`). Gives an intentional, hand-bound paper feel while remaining modern.
- **Cards, Panels, Modal Enclosures:** 6px to 8px (`rounded-lg`). Retains crisp geometry without sharp needlepoints.
- **Forbidden Shapes:** Full pill buttons (`border-radius: 9999px`) and soft cloud-like bubbles must not appear. Micro-avatars or circular waypoint pins may use 50% radius strictly when shape logic mandates a pure disc.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Solid antique gold `#B99868` fill with `#0B0D0B` text. Letter-spaced 0.08em uppercase or capital tracking via sans font. Padding: 12px 24px. Border-radius: 4px. Hover state: subtle brightness boost (`#C8A87A`) with zero vertical translation.
- **Secondary CTA:** Transparent interior, 1px perimeter of `rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.4)`, `#EAE4D8` text. On hover, background becomes `rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.08)` and border transitions to solid `#B99868`.
- **Text Link / Micro CTA:** All-caps eyebrow sans typography with a 1px baseline line in `#B99868` set 4px beneath the text.

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Archival Tag:** Background `#161916`, 1px border `#282C28`, text `#A8A89C`, font size 11px uppercase, padding: 4px 10px, radius: 4px.
- **Selected Tag:** Background `rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.12)`, border `rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.4)`, text `#B99868`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Container:** Background `#0B0D0B`, 1px border `#282C28`, height 48px, radius 4px, text `#EAE4D8`, placeholder `#6B6D66`.
- **Focus:** 1px border `#B99868` with no heavy outline rings; optional soft glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.2)`).
- **Date/Slot Selection:** Calendar day tiles conform to a square grid; chosen dates highlight via a warm gold accent fill `#B99868` with `#0B0D0B` numerals.

### Farm Tour & Flora Cards
- **Base Structure:** `#161916` fill with 1px border `#282C28`. Overflow hidden, radius 6px to 8px.
- **Imagery:** Image containers maintain 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio, treated with a faint 5% dark green tint layer to preserve visual harmony against `#111311`.
- **Card Metadata:** Upper label features small caps or uppercase sans tag; card title is set in serif (`headline-sm` or `title-editorial`) in `#EAE4D8`; footer specs (duration, terrain, flora count) sit above a 1px border `#282C28` separator.

### Lists & Flora Spec Sheets
- **Dividers:** Clean 1px `#282C28` horizontal rules between rows.
- **Row Styling:** Botanical common name (`#EAE4D8`, sans-medium or serif) paired with Latin binominal nomenclature (`#A8A89C`, italicized serif) on the left; plot location and harvest cycle right-aligned in monospaced or tracking sans (`#B99868`).

### Audio Guide & Tour Navigator Bar
- **Fixed Bar:** Docked at viewport base with 16px margins, height 64px, background `rgba(22, 25, 22, 0.94)`, backdrop blur 16px, 1px border `rgba(185, 152, 104, 0.2)`. 
- **Playback Controls:** Minimalist vector play/pause glyphs rendered in antique gold; scrub bar rendered as a hairline track (`#282C28`) filled with `#B99868`.
